6.1Exam-style: monologue

A visit behind the scenes

You are going to give a talk about visiting a film studio. You have 1.5 minutes to prepare and should speak for no more than 2 minutes (10–12 sentences).

Remember to say:

  • why people enjoy visiting places connected with films;
  • which two Studio Tour exhibits you would like to see and why;
  • how seeing genuine sets and props differs from watching a film;
  • what your attitude to studio tours is.

You have to talk continuously.

To begin with… · What appeals to me is… · Unlike… · As for me… · To sum up…

Useful language: genuine props · immersive experience · at a leisurely pace · take a detour · bring to life

6.2Exam-style: email

Worth the trip?

You have received an email message from your English-speaking pen-friend Alex.

From: Alex@mail.ukTo: Russian_friend@exam.ruSubject: A magical day out

…I usually prefer theme parks, but my family is thinking of visiting the Harry Potter Studio Tour. I’m not sure what to expect.

What can visitors see and do there? How is a studio tour different from a theme park? Would you like to visit the Studio Tour yourself, and why or why not?…

Write a message to Alex and answer his three questions.
Write 100–120 words. Remember the rules of email writing.

A little help with your email

Start with a greeting and a friendly opening. Answer all three questions. Finish with a closing phrase and your first name.

Useful language: genuine props · replica sets · immersive experience · it’s worth a visit · at a leisurely pace
